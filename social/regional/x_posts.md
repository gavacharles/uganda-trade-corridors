## Post 2 · two corridors — media: rx1_two_corridors.mp4

Same truck. Same 440 km.

Nairobi → Malaba: 197 minutes lost, past 73,000 roadside buildings.
Pretoria → Beitbridge: 82 minutes, past 8,900.

The difference isn't the tarmac. It's what has been built along it.

## Post 3 · the roadside filling in — media: rx2_roadside_growth.mp4

East Africa's trade roads are turning into linear towns.

Built-up land within 300 m of the main roads out of Kampala, Nairobi, Kigali, Dar and Dodoma grew ~50% from 2000 to 2020. Southern Africa: ~35%.

Two-thirds of East African road length is now settlement or town.

## Post 4 · people beside the road — media: rm03_exposure.png

On East Africa's main roads, about 1,200 people live within 300 m of every km. In Southern Africa, about 270.

In the villages between towns, trucks still pass them at near open-road speed.

Result: 2.5× the road-safety exposure per km.
